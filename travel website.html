Questions

Before you ask.

What do I need for a self-drive rental?

A valid driving licence and a government ID are usually required, along with a refundable deposit. We collect these at booking, not on this form.

Are tolls, parking and fuel included?

This depends on the package. We tell you exactly what is included in the quote, so there are no surprises.

Can I cancel or change dates?

Yes, with notice. The exact policy is shared when you confirm the booking.

How fast will you reply?

Usually within a few hours during the day. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us.
